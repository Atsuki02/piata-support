piata

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · 日本語

piata ("the app") is an app for collecting the meals you cook at home as a restaurant-style menu on your iPhone. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information. There is no account, and the app never obtains your name, email address, location, or device identifiers.

2. Data stored by the app

Everything you add to the app, including dish names, photos (both the original and the enhanced version), recipes, notes, meal logs, your restaurant name and design settings, is stored only on your device. None of it is sent to the developer or to any third-party server.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the app's data is included in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple, and the developer has no access to their contents.

3. Photos and camera

The app only receives the photos you choose in the photo picker. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library.

The camera is used only when you choose "Take Photo". Photos you take are stored only on your device.

Permission to add to your photo library is used only when you choose "Save to Photos" for a menu image or a dish photo.

4. Photo enhancement

Photo enhancement only adjusts brightness, color and cropping on your device. Photos are never uploaded or edited with generative AI.

5. Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising or analytics tools (SDKs).

6. Sharing with third parties

Because the app does not collect your information, it does not share it with anyone. If you use the Share feature to send a menu image or a backup file to another app, that app's own policy applies from then on.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete dishes and meal logs in the app at any time. Deleting the app from your device removes all data stored by the app.

8. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

Please see the support page.
