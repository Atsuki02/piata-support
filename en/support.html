FAQ

How do I add a dish?

Tap the "+" button at the bottom of the screen, choose or take a photo, and enter the dish name. Tap "Add to Menu" and it appears on your menu. You can add a dish without a photo, too.

Can I undo the photo enhancement?

Yes. Your original photo is always saved unedited. Edit the dish and choose "Original" to go back to the unedited photo. On the dish page you can also tap "View Original" to compare.

How do I crop a photo?

While adding or editing a dish, tap "Crop" on the photo. Choose a ratio, then drag to move and pinch to zoom.

Can I plan meals ahead?

Yes. In the Calendar, select a future date and tap "Plan a Meal". Planned meals are shown faded until the day arrives.

How do I change the design?

Go to Settings and choose Theme or Layout. Changing the design never changes your dishes.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to an external server, and the app works without an internet connection.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?

In Settings, tap "Create Backup" and save the file to the Files app or another place. On your new iPhone, open piata and choose "Restore from Backup". If you restore your whole iPhone from iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your data comes along too.
